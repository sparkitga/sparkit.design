Pricing Questions

Clear answers before you commit.

Annual video credits give realtors flexibility across busy and slower listing seasons.

Are videos included in the subscription?

Yes. Each plan includes the annual number of short videos displayed in the pricing table.

Why choose an annual video plan?

Annual plans give you video credits that can be used throughout the year instead of requiring the same volume every month.

Can I purchase one video without subscribing?

Yes. One-time realtor reels are available. Send your listing photos and project details to ask about current one-time pricing.

What is included in the free offer?

You receive two starter listing videos so you can test the process and video style before selecting a paid plan.

Can the videos match my brand?

Paid plans can include your logo, brand colors, name, brokerage, contact information, and preferred call to action.

What types of videos can SparkIt create?

Listing launches, open houses, price updates, under-contract posts, sold posts, neighborhood highlights, and agent-brand videos.

Does video pricing include social media management?

No. The video plans cover video production. Social media management is an optional service quoted separately based on your posting and content needs.

Does video pricing include Facebook ad spend?

No. Facebook ad management and the advertising budget are separate from the video plans. Ask SparkIt about the level of ad support you need and the budget you want to use.
